# A document or a task as a PDF

**Export PDF** — on a written document's head, and in a task's ⋯ menu.

It opens a clean page beside the app and asks the browser to print it, and
the print dialog is where *Save as PDF* is, on every browser and system this
product runs on. No library and no server render: what is printed is the
body as it is drawn on the screen — headings, lists, code, pictures — so
the PDF says what the screen says, and the page's title is the PDF's name.

- **A document**: its name, who wrote it and its version, then its words.
- **A task**: its title, then a line of facts — its handle, its list,
  done, who it is on, when it is due, its labels — its description, its
  steps ticked or not, and its comments with who said them and when.

An upload already is a file and has its download; a link lives somewhere
else. Neither has an export.

The page waits for its pictures, three seconds at most, before printing,
and closes itself once the dialog is done with.
